Privacy policy

SportsHall Uploader · last updated 10 October 2026

SportsHall Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the SportsHall YouTube channel to publish to that channel. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails, manage playlists and update the description of the SportsHall channel, and to read that channel's YouTube Analytics.
  • It does not access, collect or store information about any other person, including viewers or commenters.

How data is stored

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared or sold.
  • Channel statistics are read for the owner's own reports and are not shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account › Third-party connections. See also the Google security settings page.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube API Services this tool is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data handled through them is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
